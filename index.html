Connect Drive to keep your PDFs and notes on your Drive — open them on your phone, computer, or any other device and see the same library. Until you connect, the app saves on this device only.
Google Calendar on the sign-in screen Off
Shows your current (or next) event in the “Current event” box. It asks Google for read-only access to your calendar. Turn on the Google Calendar API in your Google Cloud project first.
Always-on connection Off
Keeps Google Drive connected without asking you to reconnect every hour. It uses a small free Google Apps Script that you create once — see apps-script/HUONG-DAN.md in the project.
Pen diagnostics
If a stroke ever goes missing, open this right after and send me the text — it shows what the pencil reported and why a touch was ignored.
App password
The app asks for a password each time it is opened in a new tab. Changing it applies to this browser.
Save mobile data Off
For teaching on 4G or 5G: turns off automatic downloads (a book's page, covers), checks Google Drive for changes every 2 minutes instead of 20 seconds, and asks before downloading a book over 10 MB (30 MB when this is off).
